NOTE:

In accordance with the Policy and Procedure on Conflicts of Interest, the following close relatives of staff members, with the exception of academic and administrative-academic staff members of the University, are not eligible for employment at the University:

  • Parents (parent), children, adoptive parents, adopted children
  • Half-siblings and siblings
  • Aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews
  • Spouse or domestic partner
  • Grandparents, grandchildren

The following relatives of staff members of the University are eligible for employment at the University, but subject to full disclosure:
Daughter-in-law, son-in-law, sister-in-law, brother-in-law, mother-in-law, father-in-law.
